The sound of the whisk.

Matcha has been prepared in Japan for generations: finely ground tea, warm water and a bamboo whisk. Here in Kamppi, your host guides you through each step, from warming the bowl to finding the light, quick rhythm that leaves a fine green foam.

Prepare

Start with the bowl. Your guide shows you how to bring the fine green tea and warm water together.

Whisk

Listen for the soft swish of bamboo. As you whisk, tiny bubbles gather across the bright green surface.

Sip

Cup the warm ceramic in your hands. Take a sip through the fine foam and taste the tea you’ve just made.
